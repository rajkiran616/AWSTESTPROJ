Internal engineering talk

Kubernetes.

Service discovery, shared storage, and secure service-to-service communication — the case for onboarding applications the right way.

01 · Why advocate Kubernetes

Onboard apps once. Get platform capabilities for free.

  • Standard deploy, scale, and health model across teams
  • Built-in naming and reachability for internal services
  • Consistent storage, secrets, and networking primitives
  • A place to attach company-wide security and traffic policy

02 · Service discovery

Services find each other by name — not by fragile IPs.

Without the platform

Hardcoded hosts, manual registries, environment-specific configs that break every time an instance moves.

With Kubernetes

Stable Service DNS, labels and selectors, and automatic endpoint updates as pods come and go.

checkout → payments.svc → healthy pods

03 · Shared storage

Shared storage when apps must share state — without custom glue.

  • PersistentVolumeClaims give apps durable disks across restarts
  • StorageClasses map teams to company-approved backends (EBS, EFS, etc.)
  • ReadWriteMany volumes support true shared filesystem use cases
  • Platform owns backups, encryption-at-rest, and capacity standards

04 · Internal communication

An internal company proxy between services — by design.

East–west

Service-to-service calls stay on the cluster network. No public internet hop for internal APIs.

North–south

Ingress / gateway is the controlled front door. Everything behind it speaks through stable internal routes.

  • One networking model for every onboarded application
  • NetworkPolicies to limit who can talk to whom
  • Fewer one-off VPNs, sidecars, and team-owned proxies

05 · Service mesh · Istio

Istio adds the security and traffic control layer on top.

  • Transparent sidecar (or ambient) proxy between every service call
  • Identity per workload — not just IP allowlists
  • Central traffic rules: retries, timeouts, canaries, circuit breaking
  • Security screening via AuthorizationPolicies and PeerAuthentication

06 · Encryption in transit

mTLS: encrypt and authenticate every service hop.

service A ⇄ mTLS mesh proxy ⇄ mTLS service B
  • Automatic certificate issue and rotation
  • Stops plaintext lateral movement inside the cluster
  • Works across languages and frameworks without app rewrites
  • Audit-friendly: policy is declarative, not buried in code

07 · The pitch

Onboard to Kubernetes. Inherit the platform.

What teams get

Discovery, shared storage patterns, internal proxying, and encrypted service communication — without each squad building its own stack.

What the company gets

One place to enforce security screening, mTLS, and traffic policy as more applications land on the platform.
